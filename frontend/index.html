About EUREKA!

EUREKA! is the entrepreneurship club's flagship startup pitching competition, dedicated to fostering innovation and supporting early-stage student concepts. Designed for aspiring founders, developers, and visionaries, the event acts as a launching pad to transition theoretical plans into scalable business products.

Participating teams will refine their products, analyze market fit, establish customer acquisition strategies, and pitch in front of a panel of successful startup operators and angel investors.

EUREKA! Event Guidelines & Rules

1. Team Composition

- Every team must have a minimum of 2 members and a maximum of 5 members.
- One member must be nominated as the "Team Lead". The Lead will be the primary point of contact for emails and results.

2. Pitching Structure

- Pitch duration is strictly capped at 7 minutes.
- 5 minutes are allocated for Jury Q&A immediately following the presentation.
- Pitch decks should cover: Problem Statement, Solution, Market Opportunity, Business Model, Product Prototype, and Team Strengths.

3. Submission Format

- Pitch decks must be uploaded in PDF format only.
- Maximum file upload limit is 10 MB.
- Submissions are unique per email, college ID, and registration team.

4. Disqualification

- Plagiarism of products or using non-authorized content will result in immediate disqualification.
